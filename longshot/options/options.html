Google Docs

Why Docs needs special handling

Google Docs shrinks any image taller than a page until it fits — a long screenshot becomes a thin, unreadable strip — and it rejects images over 25 megapixels. Docs-optimized captures are saved as full-resolution PNGs, each exactly one page tall and cut between lines of text. Insert them in order (Insert → Image → Upload, select all) or use Copy on the result page and paste.

Match your document’s page setup (File → Page setup). “Pageless” keeps one image per capture, split only at the 25 MP limit.

Permissions

LongShot has no access to your browsing until you click its icon or press a shortcut, and it never sends data anywhere — everything happens on your device.

activeTab
Temporary access to the current tab only after you invoke LongShot, so it can scroll and screenshot that page.
scripting
Runs the capture helper in that tab to measure the page, scroll it, handle sticky headers and restore it afterwards.
downloads
Saves finished screenshots to your Downloads folder.
offscreen
A hidden extension page that stitches and encodes large images without blocking the browser.
storage
Remembers these settings (synced with your Chrome profile).
clipboardWrite
Lets the result page copy a screenshot to the clipboard for pasting into Google Docs.
